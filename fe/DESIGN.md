## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the rigor, precision, and sovereign authority required for modern geospatial, land governance, and cadastral intelligence platforms. Designed for municipal authorities, survey departments, urban planners, and spatial engineers, the aesthetic bridges civil reliability with state-of-the-art computational intelligence.

The visual direction combines **Corporate Modern** with **Technical Precision**:
- **Authoritative & Institutional:** Grounded in mathematical accuracy, state record compliance, and non-negotiable legal clarity.
- **Analytical & Legible:** High-density data matrices, polygon overlays, and audit trails remain effortless to scan and parse under demanding operational conditions.
- **Engineered Restraint:** Controlled surface translucency, hairline structural rules, and purposeful accents replace decorative excess with functional hierarchy.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate institutional gravitas while providing pinpoint optical alerts for complex spatial reconciliation.

- **Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep navy slate anchoring primary typography, deep headers, top-tier institutional navigational bars, and high-emphasis states.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`):** High-clarity blue driving key user actions, spatial tool focus, active polygon perimeters, and progress reconciliation markers.
- **Tertiary (`#059669`):** Authoritative cadastral emerald reserved for verified baselines, mathematically reconciled boundary polygons, and confirmed single-source-of-truth states.
- **Neutral (`#64748B`):** Cool slate gray calibrating secondary attributes, coordinates, survey weights, and hairline partition lines without competing with map graphics.

### Named Functional Roles
- **`variance-amber` & `variance-amber-subtle`:** Used for boundary discrepancies, delta area variances, and survey sliver notices requiring operator oversight.
- **`conflict-rose` & `conflict-rose-subtle`:** Identifies severe overlap conflicts, title mismatches, and failed topology checks.
- **`blueprint-blue` & `blueprint-border`:** Forms the foundational surface backing for reconciliation summary banners, algorithmic weight cards, and inspection workspace panels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the balanced structural neutralism of **Inter** for narrative guidance, workflows, and structural headers with the mathematical fidelity of **JetBrains Mono** for cadastral plot IDs, area units ($m^2$, hectares), projection codes (e.g., `EPSG:4326`), confidence intervals, and spatial coordinates.

- Section overlines, category breadcrumbs, and pipeline headers leverage `label-spatial-header` in uppercase format to convey command-center precision.
- Numeric attributes, variance measurements, and geometric metrics are strictly rendered in `label-data-mono` or `label-badge` to prevent tabular misalignment and tabular drift.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a rigorous 12-column fluid grid system with controlled maximum content widths ($1280\text{px}$ for content blocks, $1440\text{px}$ or full-bleed with constrained safe gutters for spatial inspection canvas layouts).

- **Margins & Safe Zones:** Outer canvas boundaries enforce `margin-desktop` ($3\text{rem}$) down to `margin-mobile` ($1.25\text{rem}$).
- **Section Spacing Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` scaled through multiples of 40–80px) visually delineate disparate analytical workflows (Ingestion $\rightarrow$ Validation $\rightarrow$ Harmonization $\rightarrow$ Sign-off).
- **Component Packaging:** Compact data clusters (such as multi-source ingest cards and discrepancy banners) rely on internal paddings between `space-sm` and `space-md` with strict outer gaps defined by `space-lg` to retain high density without informational fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **low-contrast architectural outlines and subtle ambient layering**, preserving map readability and high contrast:

- **Surface Tier 0 (Canvas Base):** Pristine slate background (`#F8FAFC`) providing pure contrast against white working planes.
- **Surface Tier 1 (Analytical Cards & Modules):** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a sharp $1\text{px}$ hairline outline (`#E2E8F0`). Elevated with an ultra-diffused, cool ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Tier 2 (Interactive Overlays & Verification Stages):** Layered inspection cards and modal toolbars feature backdrop-filtered glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)`, rimmed by `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)` and floating elevation `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Active Spatial States:** Selected polygon parcels or reconciliation cards cast an intentional subtle electric accent glow: `0 0 0 1px #2563EB, 0 4px 12px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts **Level 2 (Rounded)** with purposeful variation based on component scale:

- **Primary Cards & Geospatial Containers:** Standard `0.5rem` (8px) radius with crisp hairline borders, imparting structural discipline without sterile sharpness.
- **Verification Banners & Action Strips:** `0.5rem` (8px) to align consistently with parent analytical views.
- **Buttons, Toggle Switches, & Interactive Inputs:** `0.375rem` (6px) to `0.5rem` (8px) for precise target perception.
- **Cadastral Badges, Metadata Tags, & Status Pills:** Full pill rounding (`9999px`) or compact `0.25rem` (4px) micro-rectangles for monospaced plot coordinates, distinguishing discrete metadata tokens from functional container cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Filled with `primary_color_hex` (`#0F172A`) or `secondary_color_hex` (`#2563EB`), text in pure white, weight 600, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, subtle hover lift with shadow transition.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** White background with `$slate-border` (`#E2E8F0`), text in `#0F172A`, hover background `#F8FAFC`.
- **Spatial Utility Button:** Translucent white surface (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.9)`), border `#E2E8F0`, monospaced plot action icons.

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- **Status Indicators:** Pill-shaped or micro-rounded with subtle tinted backgrounds and contrasting saturated borders.
  - *Cadastral Source Chip:* Background `#EFF6FF`, border `#BFDBFE`, text `#1D4ED8`.
  - *Municipal Property GIS Chip:* Background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#047857`.
  - *Variance / Alert Chip:* Background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#B45309`, with a leading alert dot indicator.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- Focus state: Outline `2px solid #2563EB` with `0 0 0 3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`.
- Integrated coordinate inputs feature mono-styled affix tags (e.g., `LAT`, `LON`, `EPSG`).

### Cards & Analytical Panels
- Structural white surface with crisp `#E2E8F0` border and `space-md` to `space-lg` internal padding.
- Multi-Source Ingestion Cards feature a designated top metadata bar with source indicator, weight coefficient, plot number, and coordinate baseline summary.

### Interactive Cadastral Map Viewport & Overlays
- Vector map canvas container with embedded floating controls (zoom, layer toggle, projection switch).
- Semi-transparent comparison overlays with color-coded vector strokes: cadastral boundary (emerald dashed/solid), municipal GIS (blue stroke with stippled fill), and variance delta zones highlighted in amber hatch.

### Discrepancy & Reconciliation Notification Banners
- Full-width interior alert panels with `0.5rem` rounded corners, tinted backgrounds (`#FFFBEB` or `#EFF6FF`), sharp borders, and inline action triggers ("Reconcile Geometry", "Inspect Sliver Delta").
